Executive Summary

204 customers churned taking ₦0.26M in revenue with them. But they didn’t leave because of who they were—they left because of how they felt: disengaged, under-served, and unnoticed.

This analysis reveals that churn isn’t driven by income or gender; it’s driven by low interaction and weak platform engagement. The opportunity? Reconnect. By targeting silent users, improving cross-platform experiences, and predicting churn early, the business can turn quiet losses into loyal wins.

The Business Problem

Despite a growing customer base, the business is experiencing a troubling churn rate of 20.4%, resulting in a direct revenue loss of ₦0.26 million. This attrition threatens long-term profitability and customer lifetime value.

The absence of clear, traditional churn drivers—such as income level or gender—made it difficult to target retention efforts effectively. Without a data-driven understanding of why customers leave, the business risks continued erosion of its active user base and revenue potential.

Data & Methodology

I approached this analysis with a structured, four-stage data-driven methodology:

  • Data Cleaning & Preparation: Corrected missing values, standardized date formats, and ensured numeric data integrity across 1,000 customer records.
  • KPI Selection: Segmented metrics across Demographics (Age, Gender, Income), Transactions (Spend, Volume, Average Order Value), and Engagement (Login Frequency, Recency, Service Usage).
  • Exploratory Data Analysis: Leveraged column charts, heatmaps, and transactional breakdown ratios to isolate behavioral anomalies.

Key Findings & Insights

The numbers didn’t shout; they whispered. Out of 1,000 customers, 204 quietly exited:

  • Engagement was the tipping point: Churned customers barely interacted. Their transaction volumes were low and their digital footprints faint.
  • Spending missed opportunities: ₦0.26M walked out the door with them—representing 20% of total customer lifetime spending.
  • Platform usage was flat: Mobile, web, and online banking performed identically. The issue wasn't where they engaged, but how they felt when they did.
  • Seasonal inflection points: Engagement spiked in January and February, but inactive customers weren't nurtured effectively to sustain long-term retention.

Actionable Recommendations

  • Reignite Engagement: Implement automated, behavior-triggered outreach for low-interaction customers before silence turns into attrition.
  • Elevate Cross-Platform UX: Streamline friction points across mobile, web, and online banking touchpoints to build sticky digital habits.
  • Incentivize Loyalty: Introduce targeted perks and seasonal rewards for consistent, long-term activity.
  • Deploy Early Warning Signals: Build predictive churn scoring models based on transaction recency and login drops to enable proactive intervention.

Impact & ROI Projections

  • Retention Value: A modest 5% reduction in churn recovers ₦65,000 directly to the bottom line without additional acquisition spend.
  • Engagement Lift: Increasing overall user interaction by 10% is projected to drive an annual revenue lift exceeding ₦100,000.
  • Predictive Intervention: Converting just 20% of identified high-risk churn candidates delivers an estimated 4x return on retention efforts.

Conclusion

Churn didn’t knock loudly; it slipped out the back door. But now we know how it moves.

This analysis uncovered a fundamental truth: customers don’t leave because of who they are—they leave because they don’t feel seen, valued, or engaged. By replacing guesswork with behavioral signals, we can transform churn from a threat into a high-margin growth engine.
