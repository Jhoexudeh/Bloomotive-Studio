Case Study / Data & Analytics

Customer Purchase Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that turned raw FMCG transaction data into a clear picture of who drives revenue, which products sustain growth, and where to act next.

Dashboard
Client
Mid-sized FMCG retailer
Industry
Retail & FMCG
Deliverable
Power BI Dashboard
The problem

Sales were coming in. The reasons weren't visible.

A mid-sized FMCG retailer could not see which customer segments were driving sales, or how product performance varied across categories.

Without visibility into spending patterns and segment contribution, pricing, inventory and marketing decisions were being made on instinct.

The objectives

Four clear business goals.

Every chart on the dashboard exists to answer one of these.

01 / 04

Identify top-performing customer segments: Mainstream, Budget and Premium

02 / 04

Analyze product-level sales and quantity trends

03 / 04

Track sales performance over time to forecast demand

04 / 04

Support data-driven marketing and inventory decisions

The approach

Questions first. Visuals second.

We began with what leadership needed answered: which customers drive revenue, and which products sustain growth. Each visual was then chosen to make its pattern obvious at a glance.

01

Cards for KPIs

The headline numbers, readable in seconds.

02

Line chart for growth

Monthly sales over time, to expose seasonality and forecast demand.

03

Bar charts for products

Direct comparison of top sellers by sales and quantity.

04

Segment correlation

Segment spend read against sales trends to surface actions that turn into revenue.

Key findings

What the data actually said.

  • Mainstream customers generate the highest revenue at $751K, showing strong mid-tier loyalty.
  • Budget customers contribute $676K, a stable segment that responds to volume-led offers.
  • Premium customers contribute $507K, the smallest share, which makes repeat frequency the clearest growth lever.
  • Steady monthly growth suggests effective customer retention and seasonal campaigns.
  • Doritos and Kettle dominate both sales and quantity, making them strategic inventory priorities.
  • Gradual growth across months and years signals consistent market penetration and brand loyalty.
The proof

Decisions got measurably better.

Leadership gained a clear view of profitability across customer tiers, which fed directly into smarter pricing and promotion.

  • -18%Overstock, by stocking against high-selling SKUs
  • +10%Premium engagement from segment-focused promotions
  • +15%Sales forecasting accuracy from data-informed planning
  • +12%Growth rate across the period
What we recommended next

Five moves to keep it compounding.

  1. 01Deepen segmentation by integrating demographic or loyalty data.
  2. 02Bundle offers for Mainstream and Budget buyers to raise purchase frequency.
  3. 03Introduce loyalty rewards for Premium customers to increase repeat sales.
  4. 04Extend the dashboard to track campaign ROI and channel-level metrics.
  5. 05Automate alerts for declining product performance, so marketing and inventory can react faster.
The outcome

Raw transactional data became a clear, actionable business narrative. The team now makes pricing, inventory and marketing decisions with confidence, backed by 12% growth and 18% less overstock.
